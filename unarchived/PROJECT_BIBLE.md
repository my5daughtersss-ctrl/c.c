### The big idea: "fortune" has two meanings
**Fortune = money** (the estate, the inheritance, the dollars) **and fortune = fate** (luck, destiny, the rise and the fall). Every story covers both: *the fortune they had, and the fortune that found them.* The money side carries the finance lessons and the affiliates, and the fate side carries the human tragedy that drives views. A viewer should see both meanings at a glance in the logo, the banner and the thumbnails.

- **Visual rule: gold light = money, cold shadow = fate.** Every brand asset shows the turn from warm gold to cold shadow. That's the rise and the fall.
- **Core symbol: the Fortune Coin.** A gold coin whose face is engraved as the **Wheel of Fortune** (*Rota Fortunae*, the old symbol of fate that lifts kings up and throws them down). One half is polished, glowing gold, and the other half is tarnished and in shadow, with one hairline crack. So the coin is the money, the wheel is the fate, and the crack is the ruin.

## 5. STYLE DNA (from 4 Undertold transcripts: Genain quadruplets, Rose Williams, Lucia Joyce, Camille Claudel)
- **Hook (first 45–70 seconds):** a date and place scene → a contrast of 3–5 escalating short sentences → a famous-name anchor → a betrayal reveal → a mystery line → title drop: "This is the tragic story of [Full Name], [epithet]."
- **11-beat skeleton:** cold open (3%) → birth, era panorama, family sketches (8%) → innocence image, flagged "Hold on to that picture" (3%) → golden moment (10%) → planted motifs throughout → behind the façade (12%) → the descent with era context (15%) → the family decision plus a fair-witness pause (12%) → long aftermath, deaths in sequence, irony (15%) → second silencing and reclamation (10%) → bookend, 1–3 lessons, CTA (7%).
- **Rhythm:** long flowing sentence then a short punch; stacks of 3 fragments; anaphora in the finale; present-tense scene drops; parallel contrasts.
- **Tone:** compassionate, investigative, fair to everyone; admits uncertainty ("We don't know"); no gore.
- **Transitions:** time jumps; "But that was only the beginning"; callback hinges; context detours; "Let's pause here for a moment."
- **Retention:** open loops; motif callbacks every 5–8 minutes; chapter cliffhangers; "picture this" immersion; moral pauses; mini-reveals every ~3 minutes; the fair-witness twist; a bookend ending.
- **Added retention rules:** payoff preview in the first 30 seconds; stakes within 20 seconds; always 2 open loops; a re-hook every 90–120 seconds; **mid-video bomb at 40–55%**; today's-money conversions; real document quotes; no "finally" before the end; a next-file tease in the last 20 seconds.
- **Direct address:** "you", "let's", rhetorical questions; CTA with a binary moral question plus "I read every single one."
- **Pace:** about 2.4–2.5 words per second (145–150 wpm). **Target about 4,800 words (±5%: 4,560–5,040), about 32–33 minutes.**

### Unarchived additions
- A money thread in every beat (dollar values, trusts, estates, who inherited what).
- **File Lesson** (60–90 seconds, about 180 words) inside the final beat, before the CTA: general estate education ending with "speak with an estate attorney."
- One soft mention per video, **never in the first 10 minutes**: the free checklist (10–15 seconds) plus a biography credit link.
- **Fact-check every script:** 2 independent sources per claim; one source means "according to…" wording; none means cut it.

## 6. VISUAL AND AUDIO RETENTION (for STATE 7–8)
- Shots of 4–8 seconds; **nothing is ever still** (slow push-ins and pans, 2.5D parallax).
- Colorized close-ups mixed with B&W archive; document overlays with highlighted lines; an on-screen **"fortune counter"** showing today's dollar value; a family-tree graphic that updates; map lines and a timeline ribbon.
- Chapter transitions: a wax seal breaking, a drawer sliding open, a "FILE" stamp, redaction being revealed.
- First frame matches the thumbnail within 5 seconds.
- Audio: music low under the voice, 1–2 seconds of silence before big reveals, subtle ambient sound, one warm consistent narrator, accurate captions (SRT).
- **Avoid:** fast cuts, meme zooms, bouncing captions, a whoosh on every cut, saturated colors, music louder than the voice, obviously fake or inconsistent AI images.
- **Targets:** 70%+ still watching at 30 seconds; 35–45% average viewed.
- **Observed reference style** (Undertold and Gilded Fate frames): grayscale and sepia archival photos, colorized portraits, B&W mansion aerials, newspaper clippings, large white captions on a dark bar, 58-minute chapters (Gilded Fate).
